## The entry

One picture of food, judged purely on how delicious it looks. The entry is a
close-up photograph-style shot of a single bowl of steaming ramen: glossy
golden broth, noodles swirled high, a halved soft-boiled egg with a jammy
yolk, glistening chashu, fresh scallions and a sheet of nori, filling the
whole frame under warm appetizing light. No text, no hands, no clutter —
just one bowl that makes you hungry at first glance.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the shipped
`entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` as `entry.png`, byte-identical.
