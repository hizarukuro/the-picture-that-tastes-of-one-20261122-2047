One picture of food, judged purely on how delicious it looks. The entry is a
close-up photograph of a juicy grilled cheeseburger on a toasted brioche bun —
melted cheddar dripping down the sides, glossy barbecue sauce, fresh lettuce
and tomato, a whisper of steam, warm golden light. One hero dish, filling the
frame, nothing else to distract from it.

`main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only) copies the shipped `entry.png`
into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` byte-identically.
